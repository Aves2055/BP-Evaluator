# BP & Pulse Meter Evaluator

A clean, responsive, privacy-first web application that evaluates **Systolic (SYS)**, **Diastolic (DIA)**, and **Pulse (BPM)** readings from any digital or manual blood pressure monitor.

## Features
- **Complete Reading Breakdown:** Evaluates overall BP category (Low, Normal, Elevated, Stage 1, Stage 2, Hypertensive Crisis) alongside individual SYS, DIA, and Pulse assessments.
- **Clinical Calculations:** Automatically calculates **Pulse Pressure (PP)** and **Mean Arterial Pressure (MAP)**.
- **Visual Spectrum Gauge:** Color-coded indicator showing where your reading stands.
- **Actionable Health Tips:** Tailored guidance and emergency warnings based on your specific numbers.
- **Local Reading History:** Saves your last 10 readings in your browser (`localStorage`) without sending health data to any external server.

## How to Run
1. Extract this ZIP folder.
2. Double-click `index.html` to open it in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) on desktop or mobile.
