    Category SYS (mmHg) Logic DIA (mmHg)
    Low (Hypotension) Below 90 OR Below 60
    Normal 90 – 119 AND 60 – 79
    Elevated 120 – 129 AND Below 80
    High BP (Stage 1) 130 – 139 OR 80 – 89
    High BP (Stage 2) 140 – 180 OR 90 – 120
    Hypertensive Crisis Higher than 180 OR Higher than 120

    Normal Resting Adult Pulse: 60 – 100 BPM (Beats Per Minute). Below 60 BPM is Bradycardia (can be normal in athletes); above 100 BPM at rest is Tachycardia.
